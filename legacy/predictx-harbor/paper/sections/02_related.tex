\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}
\para{Deep learning for tabular clinical data.}Feature-token transformers (TabTransformer, FT-Transformer \cite{tabtransformer,gorishniy2021}), attentive feature selection (TabNet \cite{tabnet}) and graph attention \cite{gat} learn interactions among heterogeneous columns, while gradient-boosted trees remain strong reference learners on small tables \cite{grinsztajn2022,xgboost,catboost}. EHR-specific models add structure to attention: the Graph Convolutional Transformer uses encounter structure as an attention prior \cite{gct}, and Graphormer adds structural biases to attention logits \cite{graphormer}. HARBOR combines a clinical prior on the attention logits with an explicit availability channel.

\para{Learning with missing values.}When values are missing not at random (MNAR), the Bayes predictor depends on the missingness pattern \cite{littlerubin}. NeuMiss derives network architectures from the conditional expectation under missingness \cite{neumiss}, Le Morvan et al.\ characterize when impute-then-predict is consistent \cite{lemorvan2021}, and GRU-D models informative missingness in time series through learned decay \cite{grud}. HARBOR propagates availability through a dedicated message-passing channel so that the mask modulates value propagation.

\para{In-context tabular foundation models and ensembles.}TabPFN is a transformer meta-trained on synthetic data drawn from a prior over structural causal models; it approximates the posterior predictive distribution in a single forward pass \cite{tabpfn}, and its second version extends the approach to larger tables and categorical features \cite{tabpfn2}. Combining dissimilar learners reduces variance and guards against misspecification \cite{superlearner,deepensembles}, and proper scoring rules make such combinations analyzable \cite{gneiting}.

\para{Active feature acquisition.}Budgeted, sequential test ordering has been formulated as reinforcement learning with set encoders \cite{shim2018} and as information-theoretic acquisition with partial variational autoencoders \cite{eddi}. Our controller uses a non-parametric conditional surrogate and a decision-theoretic utility, which suits cohorts of a few hundred patients.

\para{Evaluation practice.}Reported accuracies on the public disease cohorts vary widely, in part because of leakage through preprocessing or oversampling before splitting and through pooling of overlapping cohorts \cite{kapoor2023}. We re-implement the standard comparators under a single leakage-free protocol, follow TRIPOD+AI reporting guidance \cite{tripod}, and reproduce the strongest published benchmark protocol where one exists.
