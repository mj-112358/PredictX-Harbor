\section{Conclusion}
PredictX-HARBOR couples a typed, availability-aware biomarker representation with a two-channel graph encoder, a heterogeneous ensemble and a value-of-information acquisition controller, and serves the resulting models through validated clinical input forms. Across five disease cohorts the ensemble attains the highest coronary AUROC (0.915), is within 0.002 of the best learner on the remaining cohorts, is significantly better in 12 of 50 paired comparisons and significantly worse in none, and has a lower Brier score than the best literature-standard method on every cohort. Under the published benchmark protocol it sets the highest breast and liver results, and for coronary disease it reduces testing cost by 47\% while retaining 98\% of full-panel discrimination. The next step is a multi-hospital study whose assay, timing and quality metadata engage the quality-conditioned gate and the reviewed graph prior of Section~\ref{sec:harbor}.
