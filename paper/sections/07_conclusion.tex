\section{Conclusion}
SweepScreen turns a handheld ultrasound sweep into a per-woman screening decision with components that each do one job and can each be measured: a quality and coverage gate, a frozen-encoder lesion detector that trains on a CPU, a malignancy head, persistence-based aggregation, and a threshold that is certified at the deployment site from a stated number of local cases. All five public datasets used here were acquired by specialists on mid- or high-end systems in women already referred for imaging, and none contains whole-breast sweeps of asymptomatic women. The next step is collecting health-worker sweeps on low-cost handheld probes, which the pathway in Appendix~\ref{app:pathway} specifies. The certification procedure then sets the operating threshold for that population from local data.
